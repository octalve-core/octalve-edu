## 1. Objective

Teachers of a school write reusable questions, a reviewer assigned by the school approves them, and the questions can be browsed, kept secret from pupils until a person with authority releases them, and illustrated, exactly as in AlEemaan, but tenant-scoped: every row belongs to one school, a school's bank never crosses into another's, and the platform administrator reads none of it. This task is the first two slices of the design (the foundation, and the bank core: write, review, read, pictures, screens). Use in assessments, pupil practice, reports, forms, marking and printing are later slices.

## 2. Context & Related Docs

- `docs/development-history/forms-and-question-bank-parity.md`: how every AlEemaan idea maps onto Octalve, the rules every table and route must follow (RLS, composite keys, `auth.tenant.run`, ADR 0001 to 0004), and decisions D1 to D6 (all decided; D4: the setting `bank.parents.view`, one of `NONE`, `SUMMARY`, `FULL`, default `NONE`, used from slice 4).

Where the code is the same it is copied (`labels`, the settings rules, the secrecy rules in `rules.ts`, `overlap`, `images`, `item-input`, the storage adapters) with its unit tests; where it touches data it is rewritten for tenancy.
- ADR 0016 (the platform administrator) and 0017 (support access): no question bank route declares an `area`, so a support session reaches none of it, and a test proves it.

### Review of slices 3 to 10 (PR #79) and the fixes made on its branch (2026-10-28)

A read of the ported slices (against the fixes already made in AlEemaan) found that Octalve carried the same bugs, two more of its own, and one dead feature. They are fixed on the PR's branch, each with a test that failed first on a production build of the unfixed code (`TEST_LANE=2`):

- **The public survey link could not work.** `forms/public/[token]` read and wrote the form tables through the bare database client with no school selected, which row-level security answers with no rows and refuses every write; it also ignored the school in the address, and had no limit on requests. Now: migration `20261102150000_questionnaire_public_share` adds `app_share_token()` and a SELECT-only policy `form_public_share` on `Form` (one PUBLISHED questionnaire, by its share token: the shape of the certificate and invitation links); `forShareToken(token)` reads the survey, then the school context is set from the row it found (`trustedTenantId`, like an accepted invitation) and the questions and the answer go through the school's own policies. The school in the address must own the survey and not be suspended (else the same `404` as an unknown token); reads and answers are limited per address (120 and 60 per 5 minutes; a class on one school connection fits).
- **A guardian or a pupil could read any pupil's practice history.** `GET portal/practice/history?studentId=` returned the student of that id with no check who asked (any pupil, any guardian of the school). `getStudentForViewer` now decides: a pupil their own record only, a guardian the pupils an APPROVED link names (`portal-helpers` was catalogued as doing exactly this and did not), an administrator any; anyone else's id is `404`.
- **Printing** took any question of the school by id: now `staffMaySee` (a draft that is not yours, a secret question of a subject you do not teach, another campus's question are `404`), and a form or a quiz of another campus is `404` (`mayReachCampus`). A refusal logs nothing.
- **Non-teaching staff** could build, close, mark, print and read pupils' answers: the routes named `BANK_STAFF_ROLES` while the catalog said teaching staff. Forms routes now use `FORM_STAFF_ROLES` (ADMIN, CAMPUS_ADMIN, TEACHING_STAFF); non-teaching staff keep the question bank (review), the list and detail of forms, the print log and the reports.
- **The daily cap** counted answered questions only (any number of sets could be opened before answering one): now answers plus the unanswered part of open sets, under a per-pupil lock; **two answers to one question** both stored: now checked and written under a per-set lock; an answer is refused once the school switches pupil access off; a pupil with **no class** was offered the questions written for any class.
- **Anonymous questionnaires** kept the pupil's id and the same instant on the participation, the response and its answers (a join re-identified everyone): now an HMAC participation code under a key derived from `MFA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (the same for one pupil and survey, different across surveys), the day only on all three records, and a second or simultaneous answer is `400 ALREADY_SUBMITTED`. **`bank.smallSummary.min` was never read:** an anonymous questionnaire's results are now withheld below it, counting the pupils who answered.

### The campus rule for forms and a grant for non-teaching staff (2026-10-28, same branch)

The forms module was campus-blind on access by id (about 40 lookups used the school only): a teacher or campus administrator of one campus could read, update, close and print another campus's forms and read their results. The maintainer chose the rule ("go on the table") and confirmed that non-teaching staff do not read form results, answers and turn-ins "unless granted access by admin". One pure function, `mayAccessForm(viewer, form, "view" | "read" | "manage")` in `question-bank/rules.ts`, now decides every route and service that names a form; `lib/forms/access.ts` loads the facts inside the request's transaction (`reachForm`).

| Level | What it covers | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| view | the form and its settings, the questions WITHOUT their answer key, the lists, a BLANK printed copy | the school's administrator; the staff of the form's campus; for a whole-school form (no campus), any staff |
| read | results, answers, turn-ins, marking queues, questionnaire analytics | the school's administrator; the administrator of the form's campus; the form's creator while they still teach there; a holder of the grant (below) |
| manage | edit, close, mark, record turn-ins, sync the roll; the answer key (the correct answers and explanations, in the form, in the answers and in a printed copy) | the same people as read, except the grant holder |

- **Another campus is `404`, the same as an id that does not exist.** A non-teaching member of staff without the grant is still `403` at the door of read and manage routes, as before. A whole-school form (no campus) is read and managed only by the school's administrator and by its creator when the creator's membership covers the whole school, because its results hold the pupils of every campus.
- **The grant is a fifth extra capability, `CAN_VIEW_FORM_RESULTS`** ("See form results" on the members screen), given and taken away by the school's administrator only (the existing members API, audited as `MEMBER_PERMISSIONS_CHANGED`). It opens the READ routes only and is held to the campus rule: a holder bound to a campus reads that campus's forms; a whole-school form only for a holder whose membership covers the whole school. It needs migration `20261103090000_form_results_permission` (one `ALTER TYPE ... ADD VALUE`). The two lists of questionnaires and of paper work also accept it, so a holder can find the forms to read.
- **Lists:** a person bound to a campus now lists that campus's forms and the whole school's (before: their campus's only). The marking queue lists only the answers of forms the person may READ.
- **The marking routes check the form named in the URL against the response's own form** (before, the URL's form was ignored).
- **Printing and the answer key (the maintainer chose both, "go on with the decision").** A **blank copy** (printed without the answer key, or with no flag) is VIEW: any teaching member of staff or administrator who may view the form prints it, including a whole-school form (non-teaching staff still cannot print at all: the door of the print routes). A copy **with the answer key** is MANAGE, and so is the key itself: `GET forms/[id]` and the answers of a form (`GET .../marking`) return the correct answers and the explanations only to people who manage the form; a colleague, a non-teaching member of staff, a campus administrator looking at a whole-school form and a holder of the "see form results" grant get the questions, the options and the pupil's answer WITHOUT them (the fields are absent, not empty). A refusal logs nothing. No "published only" rule for a blank copy: a colleague can already view a draft, and now sees it without its key.
- **Was open, now done:** the AlEemaan decisions about manual marking, practice-set start and submit, and question reports are ported on the same branch: see "Marking, practice sets and question reports: the AlEemaan decisions ported" below. (AlEemaan's own forms routes were fixed in its PR #121.)

### Marking, practice sets and question reports: the AlEemaan decisions ported (2026-10-29, same branch)

AlEemaan fixed three groups of gaps in the code this branch ported (its PRs #121, #122 and #123); the same code was read here and carried the same gaps, plus one of its own. All are fixed on this branch, each with a test that failed first on a production build of the unfixed branch (`5d27233`, `TEST_LANE=2`). No migration: locks, not columns.

**Manual marking** (`manual-marking-service.ts`):
- Only a question set for hand marking (`markingMethod` MANUAL) is marked by hand: marking an answer the system already marked is `400 NOT_MANUAL` and changes nothing, singly or in a batch (a batch is all or nothing). A single mark now also refuses an attempt the pupil has not finished (`CANNOT_MARK`); the batch already did.
- An answer counts as correct only for full marks of a question that is worth marks (`isFullMarks`: `points > 0 && score >= points`). Before, a question worth nothing was "correct" at zero.
- The total of an attempt is worked out from the answers as they are, inside the request's transaction, under a per-attempt advisory lock (`lockAttempt`, key `mark:<responseId>`). The order is: the access check (so nobody without access can take a lock), the lock, then the attempt is read again. Before, two teachers marking two answers of one attempt at the same moment each wrote a total from a picture without the other's mark.
- A batch names each answer once (`400 DUPLICATE_ANSWER`).

**Practice sets, the pupil's routes** (`forms-service.ts`):
- **Start** refuses a pupil outside the set's audience (`403`); two starts at the same moment are one attempt (the check for an open attempt and the opening of a new one run under a lock for this pupil and this set); an open attempt past the set's time limit is set to `ABANDONED` and a fresh one is opened instead of being resumed for ever.
- **Submit** runs under the attempt's lock, with the attempt read after it: one submission succeeds and the rest get `400 ALREADY_COMPLETED` (before, both wrote their answers and the answers were doubled). The form must be a practice set (`404` otherwise: a questionnaire response could be completed through the practice route); the set must be open at the moment of submitting (`403` with the start reasons: a closed set, a set past `closesAt` and a set put back to draft took answers before); the attempt must be inside `maxDurationMinutes` plus 60 seconds for the network (`403 TIME_UP`, which was never enforced). A refusal writes nothing.
- **The campus (found here, not in AlEemaan):** the pupil's list, read and start did not look at the set's campus, so a pupil of one campus saw, read and started the practice sets of another. A set of one campus is now for the pupils of that campus, a whole-school set for all; another campus's set is `404` (`isFormForPupilCampus`), the same answer a form of another campus gets from staff.

**Question reports** (`reports-service.ts`):
- **The queue is the reviewers'.** Any member of staff could list every report, pupils' own words included. A report is now listed only for a person who may review its question (`reviewPower`, the rule for resolving it and for the queue of pending questions); anyone else gets an empty list (not a 403, as the queue of pending questions).
- **A pupil's report names the pupil's campus only once enough different pupils of it have reported the question** (`bank.smallSummary.min`, 5 by default, never below 3: the threshold an anonymous questionnaire already uses; task 0024 owed it to pupil reports); until then the reviewer sees "Pupil". One pupil twice counts once. The teacher's label is unchanged. (Octalve names the campus; it keeps no section of the class or version on a report, so AlEemaan's class-name leak and its arbitrary-version bug do not exist here.)
- **One open report per person per question** (`400 ALREADY_REPORTED`, advisory lock `report:<user|pupil>:<question>`), until a reviewer resolves the first.
- **A report is resolved once:** claimed with an update that still requires it to be open, so reviewers resolving at the same moment cannot each succeed, write an audit row and apply a re-grade (`400 ALREADY_RESOLVED` for the rest).

**Decisions (mine, the same as in AlEemaan; say if any should differ):** closing a set closes it for an attempt in flight (the attempt stays `IN_PROGRESS`, not deleted; a pupil part-way through loses it); the time limit is enforced at submit with a 60-second allowance, and a refused submit loses the answers; the audience is checked at start, not again at submit; retakes are unchanged; a report's campus is hidden below the threshold, not the report; the count is of pupils who reported that question in that campus (any status), not of pupils in the campus; the queue is empty, not forbidden.

**Found and NOT changed (needs your decision):** `applyRegrade` on a FIXED report only sets `exposedToPractice = true` on every assessment question copied from the item (any campus): the warning flag "this exam copy is in the pupil practice pool"; it applies no correction and re-grades no attempt, although the design says the reviewer is offered "apply the correction to that copy and re-grade attempts". What the correction is and what happens to stored attempts and published results is a design decision (it is also in AlEemaan's `docs/open-items.md`). And no limit on how many questions one person may report.
