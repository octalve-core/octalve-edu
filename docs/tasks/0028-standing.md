## 1. Objective
A student can be **suspended** or **expelled** and a member of staff **suspended** or **terminated**, as a real status on the school's own records that the rest of the system respects, with who decided, why and when kept for ever. Today Octalve's records have only `archivedAt` (students, staff, guardians), `TenantMembership.deactivatedAt`, and the enrolment statuses `ACTIVE`, `PROMOTED`, `REPEATED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `TRANSFERRED`, `ALUMNI`.

## 2. What is the same, and what is not (read from the code on 2026-11-04)
Same (see AlEemaan's 0045 for the reasoning): the states (a pupil `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` until a day or until lifted, `EXPELLED`; staff `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `TERMINATED`), the pure rules (`effectiveStanding`: a suspension applies through its last day inclusive; who may; the reason of 3 to 500 characters), the append-only history, no background job (a pupil's suspension is read as ended; a member's is lifted at their next sign-in), expulsion flips the active enrolment to a new `EXPELLED` value so that everything that selects pupils by active enrolment drops them, a suspension of staff reuses `deactivatedAt` (every reader already treats a deactivated membership as absent), nobody acts on themselves, the last administrator is protected.

Different, on purpose:
1. **Tenant and RLS.** `StandingEvent` has `tenantId`, RLS enabled and forced, and composite foreign keys (ADR 0001). AlEemaan's table has no foreign keys; here the records are never hard-deleted (ADR 0004), so the keys cost nothing and the tenant boundary is enforced by the database. The append-only rule is a database trigger that refuses an update and a delete, in the spirit of ADR 0003 (a rule that must not be bypassed is not left to the services).
2. **One membership per person per school.** There is no "staff suspended in one branch but not another": a membership is one row with an optional campus. So **there is no `scope`**; a suspension or termination is of the membership (and the staff record that names it). A `CAMPUS_ADMIN` acts on memberships of their own campus; a membership with no campus (school-wide) is an `ADMIN`'s alone (ADR 0016).
3. **Who may.** `ADMIN` (school-wide) everything but on themselves; `CAMPUS_ADMIN` suspends and lifts the pupils and staff of their own campus while the switch `standing.campusAdminMaySuspend` is on (the school's value AND the campus's; default on; a campus can only turn it off), never on an `ADMIN` or another `CAMPUS_ADMIN`; expel, terminate and reinstate are the `ADMIN`'s. The last-administrator guard is the existing `hasAnotherActiveAdmin`. The platform administrator has no part in it: school data is not theirs (ADR 0016).
4. **Guardians** are real records, so they can be archived, but they are not given a standing: a guardian's access follows the pupil's.
5. **Pupils and guardians sign in as users here** (roles `STUDENT` and `PARENT`, with `TenantMembership` rows), and the portal routes under `/api/v1/schools/[code]/portal/` (practice, forms) are the surface a standing narrows. Whose pupil a caller may ask about is decided in one place, `getStudentForViewer` in `src/lib/portal/portal-helpers.ts` ("decided here and nowhere else"), and the pupil's own record in `resolveStudentForUser`: both change. An **expelled** pupil is "not found" to themselves (the same answer as an unknown id), and a guardian's approved link to an expelled child still names the child but opens nothing; a **suspended** pupil can sign in and read but cannot start a practice session or hand in a form or paper (the routes that write). A standing only ever narrows. Guardians of an expelled child keep whatever finance view exists once there is one (there is no guardian invoice view in this repo yet). The exact list of portal routes to narrow is read from the code when this is built.
6. **Enrolment is per session** (ADR 0009): reinstating flips the expelled enrolment back to `ACTIVE` only in the running session. Where each module selects pupils (attendance, invoice generation, result sheets, promotion) must be re-read in this repo before building; AlEemaan's list is the checklist, not the answer.
7. **The settings store** for the switch is the generic table that task 0027 adds (P0).

## 5. Build record (2026-11-08)
**What exists.** The migration `20261115090000_standing` (additive: three enums, `EnrollmentStatus.EXPELLED`, `standing` and `standingUntil` on `StudentRecord`, `StaffRecord` and `TenantMembership` with the checks, and `StandingEvent` with row-level security and no UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE for the runtime role). `src/lib/standing/` (rules, common, validation, the pupil and staff services, the sign-in lift), `src/lib/settings/ceiling-switch.ts` (the generic switch over `TenantSetting`, also for `programs.enabled` later), and four routes: `people/students/[id]/standing`, `people/staff/[id]/standing`, `members/[userId]/standing`, `settings/standing`.

**Decisions made while building.**
- No scope. A person has one membership per school, so a suspension or termination is of the membership (and the staff record that names it); the AlEemaan "SCHOOL" scope does not exist here.
- The history is append-only by `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE ... FROM app_user` (the way `NotificationAuditLog` does it), not a trigger, so the owner role can still delete a school's rows when the school itself is removed.
- A suspension of staff deactivates the membership, as in AlEemaan. Deactivating here does not delete sessions (authority is read from the membership at every request), so neither does this.
- The lift at sign-in is in `completeSignIn`, the one place a person becomes signed in; it reads the person's own membership rows through the user context and lifts each in that school's context.
- The portal: a suspended pupil can read but not take a quiz, practise or hand in work (six write routes use `getStudentForWork`, which answers 403 `SUSPENDED`; a suspension applies through its last day and then ends by itself). An expelled pupil has no record to the portal at all (`resolveStudentForUser` and the guardian helpers leave an expelled pupil out), so every portal route answers as it does for anyone without one and never says why. **Deviation from the AlEemaan design:** the expelled pupil can still sign in (Octalve pupils are real users with a membership); they just find nothing. Refusing the sign-in itself is a decision for the maintainer. What is owed stays payable through the invoices, which this does not touch.
- Only the school's administrator expels, terminates and reinstates; a campus administrator suspends and lifts the pupils and staff of their own campus while `standing.campusAdminMaySuspend` allows it (the school's value AND the campus's; a campus can only turn it off), never an administrator, never themselves, and the school's last administrator is protected.
- A suspension's end date that is not a day ("2026-13-01") used to answer 500 (an invalid Date cannot be turned back into text); it is refused with 400 like "2026-02-30" (found by the API test).
- The five new GET routes (the three standings, the standing setting and the part-payment rule) are OUT of the platform support view's reach (`route-area-guard`): a disciplinary record and fee configuration are not grantable areas. A campus administrator's read of the two settings is narrowed to their own campus in the service (`route-role-guard`, `CAMPUS_CHECKED_ELSEWHERE`).
- Attendance rolls, invoice generation and result sheets already select pupils by an ACTIVE enrolment (read on 2026-11-08), so expelling drops the pupil from them with no change there. Octalve has no promotion rollover module to check.

**Screens and the sign-in decision (2026-11-09).** An expelled pupil is **refused at sign-in** (the maintainer's decision), with the one generic sentence of a wrong password (`isExpelledEverywhere`, checked in step 1 of sign-in and again in `completeSignIn`, the one place a session is made): "not found" would say that the account exists and why, so the answer is the same as for a wrong password, and nothing in it names the school, the pupil or the reason. It holds only when every place the person can still go is a school that expelled them (a person who is also a guardian, a member of staff or an administrator anywhere, or a pupil in good standing in another school, can sign in; each school's own pages and API still close what is closed to them there). Expelling also ends the sessions the pupil holds, when no other place is left to use them. The screens: a **Standing** section on the student's and the staff member's page (the current standing and its history, newest first, and the buttons that fit the role and the state, each asking for the reason), a **Standing** dialog in the Users list, a **Discipline** section on the Settings page (the generic switch panel), and the **notice** a suspended pupil is shown on their page of work (that they are suspended and until when, never the reason). Built for administrators and campus administrators only; not shown in a platform administrator's support view (a disciplinary record is not a grantable area, `route-area-guard`).

**Not built.** A notice for a guardian whose child is suspended. The program roles that standing pauses (task 0027, slice P2) wait for programs.
